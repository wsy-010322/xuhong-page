7 Ways Brands Use Custom Drawstring Bags: Ideas for Your Business

Published by Xuhong Bags | 2026-06-24

Explore 7 ways brands use custom drawstring bags for events, sports teams, retail packaging, welcome kits, weddings, and promotional giveaways.

Drawstring bags are one of the most versatile promotional products. They are lightweight, affordable, easy to customize, and useful enough that recipients actually keep them. But beyond the basic gym bag or swag bag, brands use custom drawstring bags in more specific and creative ways.

Here are seven real use cases to spark ideas for your next custom bag order.

1. Event Swag Bags

Trade shows, conferences, and festivals use drawstring bags as attendee giveaway bags. They are cheaper than tote bags, fold flat for easy transport, and give sponsors a large printable area for logos.

Recommendation: 190T or 210T polyester taffeta, single-color screen print, 14" × 18" size. Order 200–500 pieces for a small event, 1,000+ for a large conference.

2. Sports Team Gear Bags

School teams, running clubs, and amateur sports leagues use drawstring bags for athletes to carry shoes, uniforms, and water bottles. The lightweight design makes them easy to stuff into a locker or gym bag.

Recommendation: 210T polyester for durability. Add a reinforced bottom gusset if the bag will carry heavier items like cleats or basketballs. Custom team logo and player numbers add value.

3. Product Packaging for Retail Brands

Shoe brands, clothing labels, and accessory companies use drawstring bags instead of cardboard boxes or plastic bags. A fabric drawstring bag with the brand logo serves double duty: it packages the product and becomes a reusable bag the customer keeps — extending brand exposure beyond the purchase.

Recommendation: Cotton or cotton-blend fabric for a premium feel. Woven label instead of screen print for a higher-end look. Size depends on the product: 10" × 12" for small accessories, 14" × 18" for shoes.

4. Corporate Welcome Kits

Companies use drawstring bags for new employee onboarding kits, containing a branded notebook, pen, water bottle, and welcome letter. The bag itself becomes a practical desk accessory.

Recommendation: 210T polyester with a company logo screen-printed on one side. Neutral colors like black, navy, or gray suit professional settings.

5. Kids' Activity Bags

Schools, camps, and children's programs use drawstring bags for kids to carry craft supplies, snacks, or a change of clothes. The simple drawstring closure is easier for small hands than zippers or buckles.

Recommendation: Lightweight 170T or 190T polyester in bright colors. Non-toxic printing inks are important for children's products — confirm with the factory.

6. Wedding and Event Favor Bags

Wedding planners use drawstring bags as welcome bags for out-of-town guests, filled with local snacks, a schedule, and small gifts. Small brands and Etsy sellers use them for bridesmaid gifts or party favors.

Recommendation: Cotton or linen-feel fabric for an elegant look. Smaller sizes (8" × 10" or 10" × 12"). Subtle printing — a small monogram or event date — works better than a large logo.

7. Promotional Giveaways at Retail

Stores use drawstring bags as a gift-with-purchase or loyalty reward. A customer who spends above a certain amount receives a branded bag with their purchase. It reinforces the brand and turns the customer into a walking advertisement.

Recommendation: 190T polyester for cost-effectiveness at high quantities. Bold, single-color logo print. Order 500–2,000+ depending on promotion scale.

Material and Customization Tips

Most drawstring bags are made from polyester taffeta (190T or 210T) because it is lightweight, affordable, and takes screen printing well. Cotton and cotton-blend options are available for a more premium feel but cost more and have higher MOQ.

Printing: Screen printing works best for simple logos with 1–2 colors. For multi-color or photorealistic designs, digital printing produces better results but costs more per bag.

Closure: Standard drawstring bags have a single cord that cinches closed and doubles as shoulder straps. Some designs add cord locks for a more secure closure. Grommets at the cord entry points prevent fraying on heavier loads.

FAQ

What material is best for custom drawstring bags? 190T or 210T polyester taffeta is the most common choice — lightweight, durable, and cost-effective. Cotton or cotton blends offer a premium feel for retail packaging or gifts.

What is a good size for a drawstring bag? 14" × 18" is the standard size for most use cases. Smaller sizes (10" × 12") work for packaging accessories. Larger sizes (16" × 20") suit sports gear.

How many colors can I print on a drawstring bag? Screen printing works best with 1–2 colors. More colors add screen fees per color. Digital printing supports full-color designs but costs more per bag.

What is the minimum order for custom drawstring bags? ODM templates with single-color print: typically 100–300 pieces. Some factories accept as low as 50 pieces on standard templates.

Can I get a drawstring bag with a different closure? Standard drawstring bags use cord cinch closures. Cord locks, grommets, and reinforced bottoms can be added. Discuss specific features with your factory before quoting.

How long does custom drawstring bag production take? ODM orders with simple printing typically ship in 15–25 days. Custom designs or special materials add 10–20 days for sampling and sourcing.
