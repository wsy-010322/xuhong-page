Custom Cosmetic Bags

Makeup pouches, toiletry bags & beauty packaging — waterproof, stylish, brand-ready

Cosmetic bags demand both function and fashion. At Xuhong Bags, we manufacture custom cosmetic pouches and toiletry bags for beauty brands, subscription boxes, hotel amenities, and retail. Our production combines water-resistant materials, smooth zippers, and precise printing to create bags that look as good as the products inside them.

From clear PVC travel pouches for TSA compliance to quilted satin makeup bags for luxury beauty brands, we produce the full spectrum of cosmetic packaging solutions — all customizable with your logo, colors, and design specifications.

Product Specifications

Materials
PU Leather, Nylon (water-resistant), Canvas (with PEVA lining), Clear PVC, Satin, Neoprene
Closure Types
Metal zipper (YKK or equivalent), plastic zipper, flap with magnetic snap, drawstring
Lining Options
Waterproof PEVA, nylon taffeta, polyester, wipe-clean satin, padded foam
Size Range
Small (15×10cm) for lipsticks, Medium (22×15cm) for daily makeup, Large (28×20cm) for full toiletry kits
Features
Internal elastic loops for brushes, mesh divider pockets, wrist strap, hanging hook, gusseted bottom

Decoration & Branding

Screen Printing

Crisp logos on flat surfaces. Best for PU leather, canvas, and satin.

Debossing / Embossing

Blind deboss on PU leather — subtle, premium, no ink. Most popular for luxury cosmetic brands.

Foil Stamping

Metallic foil on PU, satin, or canvas. Gold and silver most popular for beauty branding.

Heat Transfer

Full-color detailed prints with soft hand-feel. Ideal for canvas and nylon cosmetic bags.

Woven Labels

Sewn-in brand labels — satin, damask, or cotton. The finishing touch for brand bags.

Production Process

1

1. Material & Component Selection

Choose outer fabric, lining, zipper type/color, and hardware finish.

2

2. Sampling

2 free samples, 7–10 working days. We test zipper function and lining waterproofing.

3

3. Cutting

Precision cutting of outer fabric, lining, and interfacing layers.

4

4. Sewing & Assembly

Zipper installation, lining attachment, edge binding, hardware attachment. In-line QC at each station.

5

5. Decoration

Logo application — screen print, deboss, foil, or label. Alignment verified against template.

6

6. Final QC

Zipper open/close test (500 cycles), lining leak test, seam strength test, dimension verification.

7

7. Packing

Individually wrapped in tissue paper, 50–200 pcs per export carton.

MOQ: 5,000 pcs per design. Lead time: 20–30 working days. Custom hardware (zipper pull, logo plate) may add 7–10 days.
